Majboos

Ingredient List:

  • Base: Basmati rice, chicken or lamb, ghee/butter, water/stock.
  • Beharat Spice Mix: Coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom, black pepper, cloves, turmeric.
  • Aromatics: Onions, garlic, ginger, green chillies, dried black limes (loomi).
  • Hashweh Topping: Fried onions, boiled split yellow peas, raisins, ground cardamom.
  • Daqqoos Sauce: Pureed tomatoes, minced garlic, tomato paste, hot green chillies, coriander.

Simple Steps to Make:

  1. Simmer chicken or lamb with whole spices, onions, garlic, and dried lime (loomi) until fully tender to create a rich broth. Strain and set the broth aside.
  2. Brush the poached meat with ghee and a pinch of saffron/turmeric, then oven-roast or crisp in a pan until golden brown.
  3. Cook soaked Basmati rice directly in the strained meat broth so it absorbs all the rich spice flavors.
  4. Sauté onions with boiled yellow split peas, raisins, oil, ground cardamom, and black pepper until caramelized.
  5. Plate the spiced rice, place the roasted meat on top, crown with the Hashweh mixture, and serve hot with Daqqoos tomato sauce.
